Eleads Login: Official CRM Login, SSO, Password Reset & Troubleshooting

Published in Business | Updated: October 2026 | Verified Editorial Review
Home › Business › Eleads Login

If you're searching for Eleads Login, you're most likely trying to access the automotive CRM used by your dealership to manage leads, customer records, sales activity, follow-ups, and other dealership workflows.

Elead, also written as ELEAD or Elead CRM, is associated with CDK Global's automotive CRM platform. CDK currently provides a central login area for its applications, including CRM, while its documentation also describes Elead-specific Single Sign-On (SSO) and authentication workflows.

The login process can look different from one dealership to another because some organizations use standard CRM credentials while others use Single Sign-On (SSO) configured by their dealership or IT administrator.

This guide explains how the Eleads Login process works, where to start, how to sign in with SSO, what to do if your password doesn't work, and how to troubleshoot common CRM access problems.

What Is Eleads Login?

Eleads Login is the authentication process used by authorized dealership employees to access Elead/Elead CRM.

Elead is designed for automotive retail operations and supports customer relationship management across dealership workflows. CDK currently describes its CRM offering as an automotive CRM designed to connect sales, service, marketing, customer data, reporting, and other dealership activities.

Depending on your dealership's configuration and permissions, the CRM can be used for tasks such as:

  • Managing sales leads
  • Tracking customer interactions
  • Following up with prospects
  • Managing appointments
  • Reviewing customer information
  • Monitoring sales activity
  • Managing communications
  • Reporting and analytics
  • Supporting service-related customer workflows
  • Connecting CRM data with other dealership systems

Your available menus depend on your role and the configuration established by your dealership.

Who Uses Elead CRM?

Elead is primarily relevant to automotive dealerships and their employees.

Typical users can include:

  • Sales representatives
  • BDC representatives
  • Internet sales teams
  • Sales managers
  • General managers
  • Service personnel
  • Marketing teams
  • Dealership administrators

Not every user has the same permissions.

A sales consultant may see customer and lead-management tools, while a manager may have access to additional reports and administrative functions.

Eleads Login: How to Sign In

The safest starting point is CDK Global's official application login page rather than an unofficial login page found through advertisements or third-party websites.

CDK's current login hub provides access to its applications, including a CRM login option.

Standard Elead CRM Login

If your dealership uses standard CRM credentials, the general process is:

  1. Open the official CDK application login page.
  2. Select the CRM login option.
  3. Enter your authorized username or email.
  4. Enter your password.
  5. Complete any security verification requested.
  6. Select Sign In.
  7. Wait for the CRM dashboard to load.

The exact screen can vary depending on the authentication setup used by your dealership.

Important Login Tip

Don't use a login URL copied from an old email, random search result, or third-party article if you are unsure whether it is still valid.

CDK's current official login hub is a better starting point because login infrastructure can change over time.

Eleads Login With Single Sign-On (SSO)

One of the biggest reasons employees sometimes get confused by the Eleads Login process is Single Sign-On.

CDK's Elead documentation specifically describes an SSO option for CRM users. Once SSO has been established, users may need to select the SSO option rather than entering their old CRM-specific credentials.

What Is SSO?

Single Sign-On, or SSO, allows an organization to use an identity provider to authenticate employees across supported applications.

Instead of remembering separate passwords for every business application, an employee can use an organization-managed identity.

Depending on the dealership's setup, the authentication environment can involve identity-management services such as:

  • Okta
  • OneLogin
  • CDK's authentication system
  • Other organization-managed identity providers

CDK's Elead documentation specifically references SSO workflows and explains that users who have already registered for SSO may be redirected to use the SSO login instead of their regular CRM credentials.

How to Use Elead SSO

If your dealership has enabled SSO:

  1. Open the approved Elead/CDK login page.
  2. Select the Single Sign-On (SSO) option.
  3. Enter your registered email or follow your organization's authentication process.
  4. Complete any required verification.
  5. Wait for authentication to finish.
  6. You should be returned to the CRM application.

If the system tells you that your account is already registered for SSO, don't keep trying the old username/password combination.

Follow the SSO path instead.

Eleads Login Password Reset

Forgotten passwords are one of the most common reasons employees search for Eleads Login.

If your login page provides a password recovery option, use that official process rather than asking a third-party website to reset the account.

CDK's Elead login documentation shows a "Forgot your password?" option in the traditional CRM login workflow.

General Password Reset Process

  1. Open the official login page.
  2. Select Forgot Password or the equivalent recovery option.
  3. Enter the requested account information.
  4. Submit the request.
  5. Check the email associated with your account.
  6. Follow the password-reset instructions.
  7. Create a new password if prompted.
  8. Return to the login page.
  9. Sign in with the updated credentials.

If the reset email doesn't arrive, check:

  • Spam folder
  • Junk folder
  • Company email quarantine
  • Email address accuracy
  • Organization email filters

If you still cannot reset the password, contact your dealership administrator or authorized CDK support channel.

Why Is My Eleads Login Password Not Working?

There are several possibilities.

Check for Typing Errors

Before resetting anything, check:

  • Caps Lock
  • Keyboard layout
  • Extra spaces
  • Correct email address
  • Correct username
  • Password-manager entry

A password manager can also contain an outdated password after an administrator changes your credentials.

Your Dealership May Have Switched to SSO

This is an important possibility.

CDK's documentation says that after an SSO account has been created, users should use the SSO option rather than attempting to continue logging into the CRM with their previous credentials.

If your dealership recently changed its authentication setup, ask your administrator whether SSO is now required.

Your Account Could Be Locked

Repeated unsuccessful login attempts can trigger account-security controls.

If password recovery doesn't work, your dealership administrator may need to verify or unlock the account.

Eleads Login SSO Error

An SSO error can be more complicated than a normal password problem because the CRM is relying on another authentication system.

Potential causes include:

  • Incorrect SSO configuration
  • Identity-provider problem
  • Expired authentication session
  • Incorrect email address
  • Organization account issue
  • Browser cookies
  • Security-policy changes

What to Try

First:

  1. Close all CRM tabs.
  2. Sign out of related identity-provider sessions.
  3. Open a private/incognito browser window.
  4. Return to the approved CDK/Elead login page.
  5. Select the SSO option.
  6. Authenticate again.

If the problem continues, contact your dealership's IT or CRM administrator.

CDK's own Elead documentation directs users experiencing persistent SSO problems toward customer support.

Eleads Login on Mobile Devices

Dealership employees may need CRM access while away from their desk.

Mobile availability depends on the CDK product and services enabled for the dealership.

CDK's broader CRM platform is designed to support connected dealership workflows and mobile tools.

If your organization provides a dedicated mobile application:

  1. Download it from the official Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
  2. Verify the publisher before installing.
  3. Open the application.
  4. Enter your authorized credentials.
  5. Follow the dealership's SSO process if required.

Avoid installing APK files or mobile applications from random websites claiming to be an "Elead Login app."

Eleads Login on iPhone

If your dealership has authorized mobile CRM access on iPhone:

  1. Open the App Store.
  2. Search for the official CDK/ELEAD application provided by your organization.
  3. Verify the publisher.
  4. Install or update the app.
  5. Open it.
  6. Enter your authorized credentials or select SSO.
  7. Complete any verification step.

If the app crashes or fails to load:

  • Update iOS.
  • Update the application.
  • Restart the iPhone.
  • Check the internet connection.
  • Reinstall the application if your organization permits it.

Eleads Login on Android

The process is similar on Android.

  1. Open Google Play.
  2. Locate the authorized CDK/ELEAD application.
  3. Confirm the publisher.
  4. Install or update it.
  5. Open the application.
  6. Sign in using your dealership credentials.
  7. Complete SSO or MFA if required.

If the app isn't available through your dealership's approved software channel, don't download an unofficial APK simply to bypass the problem.

Eleads Login Security Tips

Because an automotive CRM can contain sensitive customer and business information, account security is important.

Use the Official Login Route

Start from CDK's official login page whenever possible.

Don't Share Your Password

Never send your CRM password to another person through:

  • Email
  • Text
  • WhatsApp
  • Social media
  • Unverified support chats

Protect MFA Codes

If your organization uses multi-factor authentication, treat verification codes as confidential.

Avoid Shared Computers

If you use a shared dealership computer, always sign out when finished.

Don't Save Credentials on Public Devices

Browser password saving should generally be limited to trusted personal or organization-managed devices.

Watch for Phishing

A fake login page may look almost identical to a real CRM portal.

Check the domain before entering credentials.

What to Do If Your Eleads Account Is Locked

If you cannot log in after multiple attempts, don't repeatedly enter random passwords.

Instead:

  1. Stop attempting login.
  2. Check whether password recovery is available.
  3. Use the official recovery process.
  4. Contact your dealership administrator if the account remains locked.
  5. Ask whether SSO configuration has changed.
  6. Contact authorized CDK support if the dealership administrator cannot resolve it.

This is generally faster than repeatedly retrying credentials.

Eleads Login After a Password Change

If your dealership recently changed your password, some older browser sessions may continue using cached authentication information.

Try:

  1. Sign out of the CRM.
  2. Close CRM tabs.
  3. Close the browser.
  4. Reopen the browser.
  5. Visit the official login page.
  6. Enter the new credentials.
  7. Complete SSO/MFA if required.

If the system still rejects the credentials, contact the administrator rather than creating a second account.

Frequently Asked Questions About Eleads Login

What is Eleads Login?

Eleads Login is the sign-in process used by authorized users to access Elead/Elead CRM, an automotive dealership CRM associated with CDK Global. The exact authentication method depends on the dealership's configuration.

What is the official Elead login?

CDK currently provides an official application login hub with a CRM login option. This is preferable to relying on an old third-party login URL.

How do I reset my Elead CRM password?

Use the official login page's password-recovery option if available. CDK's Elead documentation shows a Forgot your password? option in the standard CRM authentication flow.

Why isn't my Eleads Login password working?

Check for typing errors first. If your dealership recently enabled Single Sign-On, you may need to use the SSO option instead of your previous CRM credentials.

What is Elead SSO?

Elead SSO is a Single Sign-On authentication method that allows authorized users to authenticate through an identity provider rather than relying solely on traditional CRM credentials. CDK documents SSO specifically for Elead CRM.

Does Elead CRM use MFA?

Multi-factor authentication can be part of an organization's login/security configuration. Whether it appears for your account depends on how your dealership has configured authentication.

Can I use Elead CRM on my phone?

Mobile access depends on the CDK products and services provided to your dealership. Use only official applications and approved access methods.

What should I do if Elead SSO isn't working?

Close existing sessions, retry from the approved login page, and confirm that you're using the correct SSO account. If the problem continues, contact your dealership's IT or CRM administrator.

Why can't I see certain CRM features?

CRM permissions vary by user role and dealership configuration. A salesperson and manager may have access to different dashboards, reports, and administrative functions.

Does Elead CRM integrate with other dealership systems?

CDK positions its CRM as part of a connected dealership technology ecosystem, including integrations with digital retail, finance, desking, customer data, and other dealership workflows.

What is CDK Global?

CDK Global provides technology and software services for automotive dealerships, including CRM, dealer management systems, digital retail, finance and insurance, fixed operations, and other dealership technology.

Is Elead the same as CDK CRM?

Elead is closely associated with CDK's automotive CRM offering. CDK's current public CRM materials describe the product as CDK CRM, while Elead remains referenced in CDK's documentation and product history.

Conclusion: Eleads Login Made Simple

The Eleads Login process is primarily about securely accessing your dealership's automotive CRM.

For most users, the process begins through the current CDK Global application login and then continues through either traditional CRM credentials or the dealership's configured Single Sign-On (SSO) process. CDK's own documentation confirms that Elead supports an SSO workflow and that users who have already registered for SSO may need to use the SSO button instead of their previous CRM credentials.

If your login doesn't work, start with the basics: verify the username, check the password, use the official recovery option, and determine whether your dealership has changed to SSO.

For persistent problems, your dealership administrator or authorized CDK support channel is the appropriate place to investigate account locks, authentication configuration, permissions, and system-level issues.

Once you're inside the CRM, Elead/CDK's broader ecosystem can support lead management, customer communication, reporting, marketing, AI-assisted workflows, customer data, and connected dealership operations.

The key is to use the current official authentication route and avoid relying on outdated login links or unofficial pages.
