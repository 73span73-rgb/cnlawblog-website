Guest Post Submission Guidelines

To ensure your article is accepted and published quickly on CNLawBlog, please strictly follow these editorial standards:

  1. Content Originality: All submissions must be 100% unique, human-written, and unpublished elsewhere. We do not accept AI-generated spam or plagiarized text.
  2. Word Count: Articles must be a minimum of 800 to 1,500+ words in length with deep insights and actionable value.
  3. Formatting & Readability: Use clear heading structures (H2, H3), short paragraphs, and bullet points. Include relevant image suggestions if available.
  4. Contextual Backlinks: We allow 1 to 2 relevant dofollow backlinks to your website or blog within the body content (non-promotional anchor text).
  5. How to Submit: Email your article draft (Google Doc or Word format) directly to plusplymarketing@gmail.com with the subject line: "Guest Post Pitch: [Your Topic Name]".
