HRMS stands for Human Resource Management System. In simple terms, an HRMS brings employee-related processes into a centralized digital platform instead of requiring HR teams and employees to depend entirely on paperwork, spreadsheets, emails, or separate systems.

This guide explains what HRMS Globex is, the types of functions an employee HR portal can provide, how the login process works, what users may be able to do after signing in, and what to do when access problems occur.

What Happens If You Forget Your HRMS Globex Password?

The HRMS Globex portal includes a dedicated password-recovery option.

The password-reset interface asks users for an Employee Code as part of the recovery process.

A typical recovery process is:

  1. Open the HRMS Globex login page.
  2. Select Forgot Password?

  5. Complete the password-reset process.

Is HRMS Globex Only for Employees?

No.

An HRMS can serve several user groups.

These can include:

  • Employees
  • HR professionals
  • Managers
  • Administrators

Each role may receive different permissions.

For example, an employee may only see personal information and self-service functions, while an HR administrator may have broader access to workforce records and reports.

Why Role-Based Access Matters

HR platforms contain sensitive employee data.

A system that allows every user to access every record would create obvious privacy and security problems.

Role-based access helps organizations limit what users can see or change.

For example:

Employee: personal records and permitted self-service functions.

Manager: relevant team information and approval workflows.

HR administrator: broader employee and HR management functions.

The actual permissions depend on the employer's configuration.
